The model

Who does what

RaaS is a white-label partnership. The Partner brings customers and distribution; every regulated activity stays with Belmoney under its NBB licence. The Partner never needs payment authorisation and does not act as Belmoney's PSD2 agent.

The Partner

Owns the customer relationship

  • Brand, app or web front-end (or Belmoney's White Label App)
  • Customer acquisition and marketing
  • API integration of pay-in and payout flows
  • Customer KYC through an approved KYC provider
  • Choice of corridors and pricing to end customers
Belmoney

Runs the regulated core

  • Customer onboarding approval and transaction execution
  • AML/CFT monitoring and sanctions screening on every transfer
  • Safeguarding of customer funds
  • Card acquiring, A2A open banking, Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • FX execution and payout through the global partner network
Transaction lifecycle

How one transfer moves

From a sender in Europe to a beneficiary abroad. Settlement to the Partner is in EUR; customer funds never pass through the Partner.

1 · Sender

EEA customer

Starts a transfer in the Partner's branded app.

2 · Pay-in

Card or A2A

EEA debit or credit card, open banking, Apple Pay, Google Pay.

3 · Belmoney

Compliance & FX

KYC check, AML and sanctions screening, FX conversion, safeguarding.

4 · Payout

Local rails

Bank account, mobile wallet or cash pick-up through local payout partners.

5 · Beneficiary

Funds received

Status flows back via API: Processing, Paid or Cancelled.
